# Spec: Metric dials

**Status:** implemented (the dials shipped on `main`, commits 4d5f865, e82e6fb, 7287759; confirmed by the user 2026-11-18. Earlier: Gate 2 plan revision 3 and 59 tasks approved 2026-11-16; Gate 1 spec approved 2026-11-15)
**Version:** 1.5 · **Branch:** alt-dashboard (user's choice; no feature branch) · **Owner:** Rafael Martinez · **Date:** 2026-11-15
**Supersedes:** §5.5 of `specs/alt-dashboard/plan.md` for the four metric cards (it still describes Calorías and Proteína as real intake that can pass 100%). During implementation that section gets a pointer to this spec; this spec does not edit it. **Amends:** `specs/assistant-data-scope/spec.md` (v1.9.2: REQ-19 links, reached-goal rule and tenth rounding, two Decisions lines).

> Over the ~150-line budget on purpose: large feature, four stories, a limits table with worked examples. The *what* and *why* only. UI copy is quoted in Spanish (Mexico). "Peso" is the card; W = the latest saved weight, G = the active goal. "The modal" is today's weight dialog.

## Overview

The four gauge tiles in the dashboard's "Métricas" card (Adherencia, Calorías, Proteína, Peso) are small rings that cannot be edited: the weight is typed into the modal, and Calorías and Proteína look like real intake although they are estimates. They become Kokonut UI detent-dials. Three stay read-only (not keyboard stops). Peso is edited by turning its dial (0.1 kg steps), the modal goes away, and the weight goal moves to a new "Meta de peso" section in Configuración with a direction (Aumentar or Disminuir) and a per-month history. `users.weight_goal_kg` stays the active goal. The assistant counts the goal as reached by the same direction rule as the card.

## Goals / Non-goals

- Goal: the three read-only cards show their value on a dial that cannot be turned (US1).
- Goal: the goal is set in Configuración with a direction, and its history is kept per month (US2).
- Goal: today's weight is edited on the Peso dial, with limits that fit the situation, one save per gesture and "Deshacer" (US3).
- Goal: the Peso card shows the goal as a mark and a line under the dial, and the assistant agrees with it on whether the goal is reached (US4).
- Non-goal: recording real intake (Calorías and Proteína stay estimates).
- Non-goal: showing past months' goals (rows are kept, not displayed).
- Non-goal: changing the KPI row (Comidas, Planes, Recetas, Compras) or the yellow "Resumen calórico" card.
- Non-goal: fixing the older e2e debt, or changing how Adherencia is calculated.

## Decisions

D1 to D16 were confirmed by the user on 2026-11-15 in the grilling session; D17 to D19 the same day after the first draft; D20 the same day after verify round 2 and the data and UX checklists.

- D1 Target is the four gauge tiles of "Métricas", not the KPI row: they are the only gauges.
- D2 Install the Kokonut detent-dial with `npx shadcn@latest add https://kokonutui.com/r/detent-dial.json`; it adds one component file and the `motion` package (which pulls in `framer-motion` and `tslib`). `motion` is pinned to the exact installed version and checked on Node 20 right after install; if it fails, stop and ask. The user first chose a hand-built dial to avoid an animation library, then reversed ("Install Motion instead. It's faster and more reliable"); the dependency is approved.
- D3 Our copy of the dial is edited: dashboard look (`--nt-*` tokens, Urbanist, each card's accent: Adherencia yellow, Calorías sky, Proteína white, Peso orange), because the stock zinc/orange does not match; a read-only state at full strength and not a keyboard stop (D20h), because stock `disabled` fades to 50% and the cards are information; a "value committed" event when the dial settles, because the stock dial reports every notch (dozens of saves per turn); a mark at the goal, replacing the ring's % progress; no double-click reset on Peso, because it would save a weight by accident. Kept (on Peso): rolling digits, notch pull, soft resistance past the ends, flick-and-coast, tick swell, keyboard (arrows, PageUp/PageDown, Home/End), wheel when focused, faint per-notch vibration on Android, reduced-motion handling.
- D4 Size: first, no page scrollbar at 1440×900, 1440×780, 1280×768, 1920×1080, 1920×800 (existing guarantee); second, dials as large as that allows, all four equal; third, the number inside the knob when legible, else above; the card name stays underneath. Pixels, and the threshold for "legible", are set in the plan after measuring, and the plan records that threshold as a numeric rule a test can check; the user sees screenshots before the final gate; compact (≤937px) keeps its 2×2 grid. Four stock 240px dials do not fit the 84px (60px) slots.
- D5 Adherencia, Calorías, Proteína are read-only with formulas unchanged and the same update conditions; Calorías and Proteína stay estimates shown in units, because intake is not recorded and in percent all three dials would show the same number.
- D6 The Peso dial edits today's weight (one record per day, browser-local day as now) and always shows the latest logged weight whatever the tab, because a week without a weigh-in would show "—" and invite a wrong save. Before the first weight: readout "—", needle at 50, because 50 must never look like a logged weight.
- D7 Peso limits (REQ-22): absolute 30–150, with 5 kg windows around W and G, because 0.1 kg over 30–150 is 1,200 notches on a 270° sweep. The user wrote 30–150 for "no goal" and accepted W ± 5 once a weight is logged. Limits change only after a save, because a mid-turn change makes the needle jump (accepted: a move of more than 5 kg takes two turns).
- D8 Drag for coarse changes plus small − / + buttons (0.1 kg, hold to repeat), because 0.1 kg is about 1px of finger travel and phones have no arrow keys or wheel. On phones only the knob captures the finger.
- D9 Save once when the dial settles after a drag or flick (D20m), or shortly after the last key, wheel or − / + step; toast with "Deshacer" that restores today's record exactly; a failed save returns the dial and shows an error toast (constitution: explicit errors).
- D10 Line under the Peso dial replaces the ring's % progress (REQ-35).
- D11 A stored weight outside 30–150: needle at the nearest end, readout shows the real number.
- D12 "Meta de peso" in Configuración because the removed modal was the only place to edit the goal and the user wants it in settings; a typed field is more precise for a value set rarely.
- D13 Goals and new weights are limited to 30–150 kg in 0.1 steps, also on the server (today 20–400); direction must agree with the latest weight; an inherited goal already passed stays valid until edited.
- D14 `users.weight_goal_kg` stays the active goal (user's decision); a new history table holds one row per user and month with goal and direction; same-month save replaces the row; no rows are created at month start; only the current month is editable; migration seeds November 2026. The user noted "month" is ambiguous, because a goal lasts until reached or replaced ("But for now keep as is"): the month is only the history key, and later months show the goal as inherited.
- D15 The assistant keeps reading the active goal; "Fijar mi meta" opens Configuración → "Meta de peso", "Registrar mi peso" still opens the dashboard (amendment recorded in the assistant spec).
- D16 The e2e freeze is lifted for this feature only; the older e2e debt stays paused. Work stays on `alt-dashboard`; no push without the user's yes. The modal and the old ring gauge (`WeightDialog`, `RadialGauge`) are removed once nothing uses them.
- D17 The assistant's "meta alcanzada" follows the direction rule, in this feature (US4): reached = at or below the goal for Disminuir, at or above it for Aumentar, as the Peso card does. Reason: counted from the first weight ever, the assistant says "¡Ya alcanzaste tu meta!" while the card says "faltan 10 kg" (first weight 60, now 80, Disminuir 70). The spec-writer suggested a later change; the user chose now.
- D18 Smaller gaps (confirmed decisions; the reasons are the spec-writer's):
  - Dial edges: (a) a scale that the normal rule leaves narrower than 5 kg gets a 5 kg window at the nearest end (30–35, 145–150), refined by D20b, because a few points are too few for 0.1 kg notches; (b) a read-only dial showing "—" has its needle at 0, so it never looks like a value; (c) no goal mark when the goal lies outside the visible scale, because a pinned mark would read as "goal at the end".
  - Dates and direction: (e) the browser's local date decides "this month" and "today", as the dashboard does now; (t) the inherited note adds the year when it is not the current year, so two Octobers are never confused; (d) a goal with no history row (legacy, or set in the database) gets its direction inferred as the migration does, so an existing goal always has a direction (the flip this causes is accepted, D20c).
  - Deshacer: (f) restoring an earlier weight outside 30–150 is allowed, exempt from the new limit (bounded by D20f), because it must restore exactly; (g) the toast lasts 8 seconds or until the next save replaces it (the timer pauses, D20l); (h) if it fails: error toast, the value stays as saved, no retry (D20d).
  - Server and input: (i) a value such as 70.55 is rejected, not rounded, because changing a number silently is worse than a message; (j) the save follows 600 ms after the last key, wheel or button step; (k) hold-to-repeat on − / +: first repeat at 400 ms, then every 100 ms; (l) − / + on phones have at least a 44×44px touch area.
  - Copy: (m) with a goal and no weight the Peso line reads "Meta 70 kg", with no "faltan", because there is no distance; (n) the line under Adherencia reads "25 de 32 comidas" (today "25/32"); (o) Calorías or Proteína without a plan target show "—" and no "meta" line; (q) kg use a period everywhere ("71.5 kg").
  - Behaviour: (p) "Quitar meta" has no confirmation, is disabled when there is no goal, and creates no history row; (r) the dial stays usable while a save is in flight, and the latest value wins; (s) with the dashboard in two browser tabs the last write wins and the other tab catches up on its next refresh.
- D19 Approved Spanish (Mexico) wording, quoted exactly in the requirements that use it: refusals and range (REQ-13), missing fields and "Meta guardada" (REQ-12), "Meta quitada" (REQ-15), buttons "Bajar 0.1 kg" and "Subir 0.1 kg" (REQ-26), weight failure (REQ-30), "No se pudo guardar tu meta" and "No se pudo quitar tu meta" (REQ-37, REQ-38), "No se pudo deshacer. Intenta de nuevo." (REQ-39), "Aún no registras tu peso." (REQ-11). Reason: the user approved this text.
- D20 Remaining edge cases (confirmed decisions; the reasons are the spec-writer's):
  - (a) Stored weights with more than one decimal (70.04) are rounded to tenths, half up, before the reached rule and the distance, on the card and in the assistant; the assistant's "Te faltan 0 kg" sentence is removed (REQ-35, REQ-42). Reason: card and assistant must agree, and "0 kg to go" beside "not reached" contradicts itself.
  - (b) Refines D18a: the 5 kg window applies only when the normal rule gives less than 5 kg of scale (REQ-22). Reason: 25 kg with Aumentar 70 should keep its goal in view (30–75), not collapse to 30–35.
  - (c) The inferred direction flips once the user passes a goal that has no history row (goal 70 at 69 kg reads Aumentar): accepted (REQ-17). Reason: it only affects goals written straight into the database; after the migration every goal has a row.
  - (d) No retry after a failed "Deshacer": the error toast replaces the "Deshacer" toast and the value stays as saved (REQ-39). Reason: one clear state; the user can enter the value again by hand.
  - (e) A device with a wrong clock: no new rule, the app trusts the browser's date as it does for daily logs today (REQ-12, REQ-28). Reason: the whole app is browser-date based; a guard would be a second source of truth.
  - (f) A "Deshacer" restore is bounded by the old 20–400, as a sanity check (REQ-32). Reason: it must restore exactly, but not accept anything.
  - (g) Refusals in "Meta de peso" show inline under the field with focus moved to it; saved, removed and failed results stay toasts (REQ-46). Reason: the user sees which field to fix.
  - (h) The three read-only dials are not keyboard stops; screen readers still read their name and value (REQ-2, REQ-43). Reason: they are information, and a tab stop that does nothing is noise.
  - (i) The Peso line drops a trailing ".0" ("Meta 70 kg · faltan 10 kg", "Meta 70.5 kg · faltan 4.5 kg"), as the assistant does; the dial readout keeps one decimal, without unit ("72.0"; v1.4) (REQ-35). Reason: one wording on card and assistant; the line reads as a sentence, the readout as a measurement.
  - (j) Smallest supported width is 360px (REQ-45). Reason: the width the project's other specs test down to.
  - (k) For "—", screen readers say "Sin datos" on the read-only dials and "Sin registro de peso" on Peso (REQ-43). Reason: a dash is read poorly or not at all.
  - (l) The "Deshacer" toast timer pauses while the pointer or keyboard focus is on the toast; other toasts keep the app's normal behaviour (REQ-27). Reason: a keyboard user needs more than 8 seconds to reach it.
  - (m) Refines D9: a flick keeps turning after release, so the save happens when the dial settles, not at release; a pending save is sent at once if the user leaves the page or the dashboard (REQ-27). Reason: saving at release would store a value the dial is still moving past; the flush means nothing is lost.

## Delivery notes

- **Story order and the goal.** Once the modal is removed the goal needs another home, so US2 (Meta de peso, assistant link, goal field removed from the modal) ships before US3 (modal removed). Interim states: after US1 the Peso tile keeps today's gauge and modal (its size is not yet equal to the dials); after US2 the modal holds only "Peso de hoy (kg)"; after US3 and before US4 the Peso card shows no goal information at all (no mark, no line, no % progress), the goal is visible only in Configuración, and the dial limits already use it. There is always one way to set the goal and one to log the weight.
- **Migration.** US2 is not usable until the migration has run, and its code and the migration land together: there is no delivered state with the Meta de peso code and no history table. Saving a goal writes the active goal and the history row together or not at all (REQ-12). The migration runs only with the user's explicit yes (Always ask).
- **Assistant amendment.** The v1.9.2 amendment of `specs/assistant-data-scope/spec.md` is bound to this spec: it describes behaviour that exists only once US2 (the "Fijar mi meta" link) and US4 (the reached rule) ship, and it is reverted if this spec is rejected.

## User stories

### US1 (P1): Read-only dials for Adherencia, Calorías, Proteína
As the user, I want these three cards on dials so that I read them at a glance in the dashboard's look.

- **REQ-1:** Each dial uses `--nt-*` tokens, Urbanist and its card's accent, in light and dark. No stock zinc or stock orange colour remains in our copy of the dial; Peso uses the dashboard's orange accent token.
  - Given dark theme, when I look at the Adherencia fill, then its colour equals the yellow accent token; Calorías sky, Proteína white; once US3 ships, Peso equals the orange accent token.
  - Given either theme, when I compare the colours a dial draws (fill, ticks, knob, text, end labels) with the dashboard's token values, then each is a token value and none equals a stock zinc or stock orange value (the plan lists the stock values).
- **REQ-2:** Read-only state: full strength (opacity 1, normal cursor), no change by drag, touch or wheel; no commit event; not a keyboard stop (Tab skips it, so no key can operate it); assistive technology reads its name and value (REQ-43) as a read-only value, not as an adjustable slider.
  - Given the Adherencia dial reads 82%, when I drag it, click it or scroll the wheel over it, then it still reads 82% and no request is sent. Given I press Tab through the card, then focus never lands on Adherencia, Calorías or Proteína. Given a screen reader's reading cursor on it, then it reads "Adherencia" and its value, and it is not announced as adjustable.
- **REQ-3:** Adherencia: scale 0–100, readout "N%" with today's formula (rounded to a whole number), the line underneath "N de M comidas" ("1 de 1 comida" when M is 1), updated under today's conditions (tab change, marking a meal).
  - Given 25 of 32 meals done in the period, then "78%" (78.125) and "25 de 32 comidas". Given I mark one more meal done, then "81%" (26 of 32 = 81.25) and "26 de 32 comidas" without reload. Given 1 of 1 meals done, then "100%" and "1 de 1 comida".
- **REQ-4:** Calorías: scale 0 → the plan's kcal target; readout "≈ N" (v1.4: no unit in the knob; whole kcal, half up, thousands comma) = the unrounded meals-done fraction × target, not the rounded percentage; "meta N kcal" underneath.
  - Given 25 of 32 meals (0.78125) and target 1,850, then "≈ 1,445" (1,445.3125; the rounded 78% would give 1,443) and "meta 1,850 kcal".
- **REQ-5:** Proteína: scale 0 → the protein target in grams; readout "≈ N" (v1.4: no unit in the knob; whole grams, half up) = the unrounded fraction × target; "meta N g" underneath.
  - Given 25 of 32 meals (0.78125) and target 112 g, then 87.5 shows as "≈ 88" (the rounded 78% would give 87) and "meta 112 g".
- **REQ-6:** No data or no target: the readout is "—" and the needle rests at 0 (D18b). Calorías and Proteína without a target also show no "meta" line (D18o). "—" is only for no logs or no target: logged days with no meal done show zeros, with formulas unchanged (D5).
  - Given no daily logs in the period, then all three read "—" with needles at 0. Given the plan has no kcal target, then Calorías and Proteína read "—", with no "meta" line, and Adherencia still shows its value.
  - Given the period has logged days and 0 of 32 meals done, then Adherencia reads "0%" with "0 de 32 comidas", Calorías "≈ 0" (still with "meta 1,850 kcal") and Proteína "≈ 0" (with "meta 112 g"), needles at 0.
- **REQ-7:** All three follow the Semana/Mes/Año/Histórico tabs; on a tab change the needle glides to the new value (instantly under reduced motion).
  - Given Semana 82% and Mes 64%, when I select Mes, then the needle glides to 64% and reads "64%".
- **REQ-8:** End labels (numbers at both ends of the ring) show only where they are legible. "Legible" is a threshold the plan sets after measuring (D4) and records as a numeric rule that a test can check; this spec fixes no value.
  - Given a dial below the plan's threshold (for example the compact layout), then no end labels show; given a dial at or above it, then "0" and the maximum show.
- **REQ-9:** Layout: no vertical page scrollbar at the five sizes of D4, with data and in the empty state; the three dials equal in size; the number inside the knob, its font size set by a numeric rule the plan records and never below 10px (v1.4); only when some dial's longest value would need less than 10px (for example a target of five digits at the smallest sizes) do all four numbers sit above, so nothing clips (REQ-45); the name underneath; the Peso tile's visible name is "Peso (kg)" (its accessible name stays "Peso", REQ-43); at ≤937px a 2×2 grid.
  - Given the dashboard at each of the five sizes, then the document is not taller than the viewport; given 390×844, then the tiles form a 2×2 grid.
- **REQ-10:** The dependency is installed with the D2 command and `motion` is pinned to an exact version (no `^` or `~`); the app type-checks and starts, and the e2e tests for this story's areas (the Métricas tiles and the five-size fit) pass on Node 20. The paused older e2e debt is out of scope (D16).
  - Given install is done, when I read `package.json`, then `motion` has an exact version; given it fails on Node 20, then work stops and the user is asked.
- **REQ-43:** Name and value that assistive technology reads from each dial, built only from strings the card shows (D20k); Peso's row applies from US3, and Peso is an adjustable slider whose minimum and maximum are the REQ-22 limits; the three read-only dials are exposed as a read-only value, not as an adjustable slider (REQ-2):
  | Dial | Name | Value | Without data |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Adherencia | "Adherencia" | "78%, 25 de 32 comidas" | "Sin datos" |
  | Calorías | "Calorías" | "≈ 1,445 kcal, meta 1,850 kcal" | "Sin datos" |
  | Proteína | "Proteína" | "≈ 88 g, meta 112 g" | "Sin datos" |
  | Peso | "Peso" | "71.5 kg" | "Sin registro de peso" |
  - Given 25 of 32 meals and the targets of REQ-4 and REQ-5, then each dial's name and value read as in the table. Given no plan target, then Calorías and Proteína read "Sin datos" with no "meta" part. Given no weight ever, then Peso reads "Sin registro de peso". Given a screen reader on Adherencia, Calorías or Proteína, then it is not announced as a slider or as adjustable, while Peso is.
- **REQ-44:** Contrast in light and dark theme (the "Métricas" card is black in both, see Assumptions): text and the end labels at least 4.5:1; fill, ticks, knob outline, goal mark and focus indicator at least 3:1, each against what it sits on; this applies to each element as its story ships.
  - Given each accent (yellow, sky, white, orange) in either theme, when I measure the fill, ticks and readout text, then the fill and ticks reach 3:1 and the text 4.5:1.
- **REQ-45:** Widths: the compact layout ends at 937px and the wide one starts at 938px; the smallest supported width is 360px with no horizontal scroll; below 1280px wide the dashboard is a single column and the page scrolls, as it does today, and the no-scrollbar guarantee applies only to the five window sizes of D4; the longest values never clip, overlap or truncate the readout, and the plan records the fit rule.
  - Given 937px wide, then the tiles form a 2×2 grid. Given 938px wide (for example 938×780), then the four tiles sit side by side, and the page may scroll vertically. Given 360px wide, then the 2×2 grid shows with no horizontal scroll. Given Calorías "≈ 12,345 kcal", then it shows in full without overlapping its dial or tile edge, at 360px and at 938px.

### US2 (P2): "Meta de peso" in Configuración
As the user, I want to set my goal and its direction in settings so that the dial and the assistant know whether I want to gain or lose.

- **REQ-11:** Configuración has a "Meta de peso" section with a two-option choice "Aumentar" / "Disminuir" under the group label "Objetivo", a number field "Meta (kg)" (30–150, step 0.1), a reference line "Tu peso actual: 74.5 kg" (W; with no weight: "Aún no registras tu peso."), and the actions "Guardar meta" and "Quitar meta". With no goal the choice and field are empty; with a goal they show it.
  - Given W = 74.5 and no goal, then the line reads "Tu peso actual: 74.5 kg" and both fields are empty. Given no weight, then the line reads "Aún no registras tu peso.". Given the section, then the "Aumentar" / "Disminuir" choice is a group named "Objetivo" (read by a screen reader with the group).
- **REQ-12:** "Guardar meta" needs both fields; it sets the active goal and the direction, and writes or replaces the current month's history row (one row per user and month), all together or not at all. The current month is the browser's local month (D18e), and the server does not substitute its own. On success the toast reads "Meta guardada". A missing choice is refused with "Elige si quieres aumentar o disminuir de peso."; a missing number with "Escribe tu meta en kilogramos."; nothing changes (where they show: REQ-46).
  - Given no goal and no weight logged, when I save Disminuir and 70 in November 2026, then the active goal is 70, one row (Nov 2026, 70, Disminuir) exists and "Meta guardada" shows. Given I save Aumentar and 80 the same month, then still one row, now (80, Aumentar).
- **REQ-13:** Validation, in the form and on the server: goal 30–150 in 0.1 steps; with a logged weight, Aumentar needs G > W and Disminuir needs G < W; refused with the message below, nothing changes. A value with more than one decimal is rejected, not rounded (D18i).
  - Given W = 74.5: Disminuir 74.4 saves; Disminuir 74.5 and 74.6 are refused with "Para disminuir, tu meta debe ser menor que tu peso actual (74.5 kg)."; Aumentar 74.6 saves; Aumentar 74.5 is refused with "Para aumentar, tu meta debe ser mayor que tu peso actual (74.5 kg)."; 29.9 and 150.1 are refused with "La meta debe estar entre 30 y 150 kg."; 70.55 is refused with "Usa un solo decimal, por ejemplo 70.5.". Given no logged weight, then Aumentar 40, Aumentar 30 and Disminuir 150 save.
  - Given a direct request with goal 20 or 70.55, then it is rejected and the goal and history are unchanged.
- **REQ-14:** A goal lasts until replaced or removed. In a later month the section shows the goal and direction with the note "heredada de <mes>" (the month of the latest row), plus " de <año>" when that year is not the current one (D18t); no row is created until I save. An inherited goal already passed stays active and valid until I edit it; the new values are then validated by REQ-13.
  - Given a row (Oct 2026, 70, Disminuir), W = 68 and today is 1 December 2026, then the section shows 70, Disminuir and "heredada de octubre", no December row exists, and the goal is active. Given I save 66 in December, then a December row (66, Disminuir) exists and the note is gone.
  - Given a row (Oct 2025, 70, Disminuir) and today is 15 November 2026, then the note reads "heredada de octubre de 2025".
- **REQ-15:** "Quitar meta" clears the active goal, empties the fields, keeps all history rows and writes none; there is no confirmation, and the action is disabled when there is no goal. The toast reads "Meta quitada". The direction applies only while there is an active goal: after removal the dial, the Peso line and the assistant treat the user as having no goal, even though the latest history row still exists.
  - Given goal 70 (Disminuir) set in November, when I remove it, then the active goal is empty, the November row still holds (70, Disminuir), and with W = 80 the dial limits are 75–85 (the no-goal rule), the Peso line reads "Sin meta" and the assistant says "No has fijado meta de peso.". Given I then save Aumentar 85 in November, then that row becomes (85, Aumentar).
- **REQ-16:** The migration creates the history table and, for a user with a goal, adds the row for November 2026: Disminuir when G < the latest logged weight, else Aumentar (equal, or no weight, gives Aumentar). The latest logged weight is the weight on the most recent date that has one (the same W as REQ-20). Users without a goal get no row. Nothing is dropped and `users.weight_goal_kg` stays. It runs only with the user's explicit yes, and it is delivered together with the code of this story (Delivery notes).
  - Given G = 70 and latest weight 74.5, then (Nov 2026, 70, Disminuir). Given G = 80 and latest 74.5, or no weight, then Aumentar. Given weights 72.0 on Nov 3 and an empty weight on Nov 10 (day logged, no weight), then the latest logged weight is 72.0.
- **REQ-17:** Every goal set or removal updates `users.weight_goal_kg`, which is still what the dashboard and the assistant read as the goal. The direction is read from the latest history row (greatest month, REQ-47) while there is an active goal and that row's goal equals the active goal; otherwise (no row, or a row whose goal differs) it is inferred as in REQ-16 (D18d). With no goal there is no direction. Interim (US2 until US3): the modal no longer shows "Meta (kg)".
  - Given I save 70 in Configuración, then the dashboard and the assistant's "¿Cuál es mi meta?" use 70.
  - Given a goal of 70 set in the database with no history row and W = 74.5, then the direction is Disminuir; given a goal of 80, or 74.5, then Aumentar; given no weight, then Aumentar. Given a row (Nov, 70, Disminuir), an active goal of 80 set in the database and W = 74.5, then the direction is Aumentar.

## Edge cases and errors

- No data in the period, no plan target: "—" and needle at 0 (REQ-6); logged days with no meal done show zeros (REQ-6). Before the first weight: "—", needle at 50 (REQ-21).
- Stored weight outside 30–150 or a scale under 5 kg: REQ-24, REQ-22. Failed save: REQ-30. Failed Deshacer: REQ-39. Failed goal save or removal: REQ-37, REQ-38.
- Deshacer when today had no record, or had one, and saving twice in a day: REQ-28, REQ-29.
- Passing the goal: the window follows (REQ-22); an inherited goal already passed stays active (REQ-14); reached state (REQ-35, REQ-42).
- Removing the goal: REQ-15. First of a month: no new row (REQ-14). Goal with no history row: REQ-17.
- Keyboard, wheel, touch: REQ-2, REQ-25, REQ-31, REQ-49, REQ-50. Widths and window sizes: REQ-9, REQ-33, REQ-45.
- A save in flight while the user turns again: REQ-40. Two browser tabs: REQ-41. Leaving with a pending save: REQ-27.
- The seed copies a goal unchanged even when it lies outside 30–150 or has two decimals; the section shows it and saving applies REQ-13. Re-running the migration never overwrites or duplicates a row. The migration only adds, so undoing it loses no existing data.
- Every existing reader of the active goal keeps its old value and meaning (REQ-17); the plan lists them.
- A device with a wrong clock: the browser's date is trusted (D20e). An out-of-date page: REQ-48. History rows are never deleted: REQ-47.

## Non-functional requirements

- Accessibility: the Peso knob, − and + are keyboard operable with a visible focus (REQ-49); name and value reach assistive tech (REQ-43); contrast per REQ-44 in light and dark (WCAG 2.1 AA, as `specs/theme-switcher/spec.md` REQ-7 requires of data visuals); results announced politely (REQ-46, REQ-51).
- Motion: honours `prefers-reduced-motion` as the stock dial does.
- Precision: weights and distances are computed in tenths of a kilo with no floating-point artifacts (71.3 − 70.1 shows 1.2); a stored weight is rounded to tenths, half up, before the reached rule and the distance (D20a).
- Format: kg use a period everywhere (D18q). The dial readout shows one decimal without unit ("71.5"; the unit is in the tile name "Peso (kg)") and Configuración "71.5 kg"; the Peso line drops a trailing ".0" (REQ-35).
- Privacy: failure logs carry the operation and the error, never a weight or goal value; nothing is sent outside the app.
- Errors are never swallowed (constitution 6). Breakpoints 937/938 only (CLAUDE.md).

## Assumptions

- Percentages round to the nearest whole number as today; weights show one decimal ("72.0 kg"); the period is the decimal point.
- "Sin meta" is itself the link; the line follows the saved weight, not the turning value; Deshacer is single-level and the next save replaces its toast (D18g).

- First render: the dials show their values directly, with no entrance animation (the REQ-7 glide on a tab change still applies). There is no loading placeholder and no busy look while a save is in flight.
- − / + are disabled at the limits (REQ-50); not decided by the user.
- A release at the saved value saves nothing and shows no toast (REQ-27); one further save follows a turn made while a save is in flight (REQ-40); leaving during a coast sends the value the needle is heading to.
